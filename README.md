# graph-embeddings

# Título y descripción

## What do Mayan Embeddings Capture?

This work addresses a central challenge in Natural Language Processing for low-resource and morphologically rich languages: the development and evaluation of semantic representations under conditions of limited data availability. Focusing on Yucatec Maya, we present the first systematic evaluation of word embeddings for this language and introduce a graph-based methodology designed to mitigate the long-tail distribution of rare inflected forms.
The study makes several contributions that we believe align closely with the scope of Language Resources and Evaluation. First, we construct and process a multi-source corpus of 717,471 tokens for Yucatec Maya. Second, we develop and evaluate semantic representations using graph topologies and compare them against widely used distributional approaches. Third, and most importantly, we create the first expert-validated gold standard for semantic similarity in Yucatec Maya through the participation of five native linguistic experts. Finally, we release the resulting embeddings, corpus, and evaluation resources to facilitate future research on Mayan and other low-resource languages.
